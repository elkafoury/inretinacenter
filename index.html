Services

Our team of experienced retina specialists use the latest techniques and technology to diagnose and treat a wide range of conditions, including macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and retinal detachment. Whether you are seeking a second opinion, a diagnosis, or treatment, our team is here to help.

Age-related macular degeneration Treatment

Our team of retina specialists uses the latest techniques, such as anti-VEGF injections, to treat age-related macular degeneration (AMD) and other forms of macular degeneration. Learn More

Diabetic Retinopathy Treatment

We provide comprehensive care for patients with diabetic retinopathy, including laser therapy, anti-VEGF injections, and vitrectomy.
Learn More

Retinal Detachment Treatment

Our retina specialists have extensive experience in treating retinal detachment, including pneumatic retinopexy, scleral buckling, and vitrectomy.
Learn More

Retinal vein occlusion Treatment

Retinal vein occlusions occur when there is a blockage of veins carrying blood with needed oxygen and nutrients away from the nerve cells in the retina. A blockage in the retina’s main vein is referred to as a central retinal vein occlusion (CRVO), while a blockage in a smaller vein is called a branch retinal vein occlusion (BRVO). Learn More

Retinal arterial occlusion

Retinal artery occlusion refers to blockage of the retinal artery carrying oxygen to the nerve cells in the retina at the back of the eye. The lack of oxygen delivery to the retina may result in severe loss of vision.
Learn More

Posterior vitreous detachment

Posterior Vitreous Detachment (PVD) is a natural change that occurs during adulthood, when the vitreous gel that fills the eye separates from the retina, the light-sensing nerve layer at the back of the eye.
Learn More

Retinal tear

The retina is the inner lining of the eye; it is the thin, light-sensitive tissue that generates vision. Tears can form in the retina, creating a risk of retinal detachment and severe loss of vision.
Learn More

Macular hole

The macula is a small area in the center of the retina where light is sharply focused to produce the detailed color vision needed for tasks such as reading and driving. When a full-thickness defect develops in the macula, the condition is referred to as macular hole.
Learn More

Epiretinal membrane

(ERMs), also commonly known as cellophane maculopathy or macular puckers, are avascular (having few or no blood vessels), semitranslucent, fibrocellular membranes that form on the inner surface of the retina. They most commonly cause minimal symptoms and can be simply observed, but in some cases they can result in painless loss of vision and metamorphopsia (visual distortion). Generally, ERMs are most symptomatic when affecting the macula, which is the central portion of the retina that helps us to distinguish fine detail used for reading and recognizing faces.
Learn More

Vitrectomy

We use this procedure to remove blood, scar tissue, or other debris from the vitreous cavity in the eye.
Learn More

Injections

Our retina specialists are experts in providing intravitreal injections for diseases such as macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, and retinal vein occlusion.
Learn More

Meet Our Doctors

Our doctors and team of dedicated staff are committed to providing you and your family with the best care, utilizing the newest methods and technology available for the treatment of retinal diseases. The Indiana Retina Center's doctors are all ophthalmologists certified by the American Board of Ophthalmology (ABO), and have additional subspecialty training in the diagnosis and management of diseases of the retina, vitreous and macula.

James D. Kim, MD MS

Fellowship trained and board certified retina specialist

Surgical Retina Fellowship: The Eye Institute of the Medical College of Wisconsin, Milwaukee.

Ophthalmology Residency: University of Texas Medical Branch.

  • Surgical Retina Fellowship: The Eye Institute of the Medical College of Wisconsin, Milwaukee
  • Ophthalmology Residency: University of Texas Medical Branch
  • Medical School: Georgetown University
  • Masters Degree in Physiology and Biophysics, Georgetown University
  • Masters Degree in Biotechnology, Johns Hopkins University
  • Bachelors Degree in Biochemistry, University of Maryland College Park

Dr. James D. Kim is a fellowship trained and board certified retina specialist. He is passionate about providing best care for patient with retinal disease. Dr. Kim had interesting life growing up and spent time living in different countries around the world. He was born in South Korea and moved to Argentina at young age.

Then he moved to Australia where he graduated high school while achieving a perfect score on the Australian University Entrance Exam and received the Premier's medal as the top student in Australia. Then Dr. Kim came to the United States and graduated from University of Maryland with a Bachelor of Science degree in Biochemistry. Prior to attending medical school, Dr. Kim received a Master of Science degree in Biotechnology from Johns Hopkins University in Baltimore, Maryland. In addition, Dr. Kim received a second Master of Science Degree in Physiology and Biophysics from Georgetown University in Washington, D.C. He stayed at Georgetown University and received his Doctor of Medicine degree.

Dr. Kim completed his ophthalmology residency at the University of Texas Medical Branch where he served as chief resident. He was awarded numerous accolades including:

  • Silver Scalpel Award for recognition of his superior surgical skills
  • Best Research Awards for outstanding achievement in furthering ophthalmology research and nominated as university-wide exemplary resident
  • Resident Citizenship Award

After his ophthalmology residency, Dr. Kim was selected as a vitreoretinal surgery fellow at the Eye Institute in Medical College of Wisconsin, one of the premier retina fellowships in the country.

Dr. Kim has extensive medical and surgical training in retinal diseases including age-related macular degeneration, diabetic retinopathy, macular hole repair, complex retinal detachments and diabetic tractional retinal detachments.

Dr. Kim has been involved in research for many years including National Institute of Health and Johns Hopkins Medical Institute. He has numerous research publication in top medical journals and book chapters. He has presented his works at national and international meetings. He is a fellow of American Board of Ophthalmology, member of the American Academy of Ophthalmology, American Society of Retina Specialists, the American Medical Association and the American College of Physicians.

Mohamed Gendy, MD FASRS

Fellowship trained and board certified retina specialist

Surgical Retina Fellowship: The Medical College of Wisconsin in Milwaukee.

Ophthalmology Residency: Northwestern University School of Medicine in Chicago.

Mohamed "Mo" Gendy, M.D., is a fellowship-trained retina specialist and a board-certified ophthalmologist who specializes in medical and surgical diseases of the retina and vitreous, including diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, retinal vascular diseases, epiretinal membranes, macular holes, and retinal detachment. He joined the Indiana Retina Center after he was practicing in central Illinois for seven years.

He completed three years of residency in Ophthalmology at Northwestern University School of Medicine in Chicago. He then went on for a two-year Surgical Retina Fellowship at the Medical College of Wisconsin in Milwaukee, which is one of the most prestigious nationally known fellowships under the teaching of the world-renowned retina specialists including Dr. Judy Kim, the current president of the American Society of Retina Specialists. There, he served as an instructor in ophthalmology, where he was actively involved in ophthalmology resident and medical student education.

Dr. Gendy’s research interests pertain to ocular imaging and clinical trials. He completed two research fellowships at the world-renowned Bascom Palmer Eye Institute in Miami, Florida. He has also published peer-reviewed scientific publications and presented his work at numerous national meetings. He has been involved in several pivotal clinical trials where he was an investigator for several phase II and phase III clinical trials for age-related macular degeneration, diabetic macular edema and retinal vein occlusion.

Dr. Gendy is certified by the American Board of Ophthalmology and is a member of the American Academy of Ophthalmology (AAO) and a fellow of the American Society of Retina Specialists (ASRS). He brings to us the latest advancements in medical and surgical treatments of retinal diseases and we are proud to bring this level of expertise to our community. Dr. Gendy strives to provide highly professional, caring, and compassionate care, and he looks forward to the opportunity to serve you.

Jason So, MD

Fellowship trained and board certified retina specialist

Vitreoretinal Surgery Fellowship: University of South Florida in Tampa.

Ophthalmology Residency: Louisiana State University School Health Sciences Center (LSUHSC).

Dr. Jason So is a board-certified and fellowship trained retina specialist. He received his Bachelor of Science degree in Biology from Louisiana State University and Doctor of Medicine degree from Louisiana State University Health Sciences Center (LSUHSC). Afterwards, he remained in Louisiana to complete his internal medicine internship and ophthalmology residency at LSUHSC where he served as the Chief Resident of the Ophthalmology Department. Dr. So then completed a two-year vitreoretinal surgery fellowship at the University of South Florida in Tampa.

Dr. So specializes in medical and surgical management of retinal diseases including retinal detachment, diabetic retinopathy, macular degeneration, retinal vein occlusion, and macular hole. His research interests include retinopathy of prematurity and secondary lens implantation. Dr. So has presented in national conferences and has participated in multiple clinical trials. He has invented and patented a novel method of secondary lens implantation.

His personal interests include health and fitness, cooking, playing guitar, traveling, and spending time with his wife and two sons.
